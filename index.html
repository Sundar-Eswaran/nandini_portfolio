About

A short version of a longer story — why I chose AI, and what I've done with it since.

The Spark

Why AI

I noticed the same pattern everywhere — messy, scattered data hiding a decision that could save time, money or even a life. AI was the first tool I found that could actually listen to that data and act on it.

The Method
